# Dictate

Private, offline dictation. Press a key, speak, press it again: your words are typed wherever your
cursor is. Speech recognition runs on your own computer (OpenAI's Whisper, via faster-whisper); your
voice never leaves it.

## Windows 10/11

Open **PowerShell** and run:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nitinreddy08/dictate/main/install.ps1 | iex
```

No admin rights and no Python needed: the installer brings its own. A guided setup then checks your PC,
shows the speech models with their pros and cons (and recommends one), downloads it with a progress bar,
tests your microphone and lets you pick a shortcut.

- **Use it:** click into any text box, press **Insert**, speak, then press **Insert** or **Enter**.
- **Tray icon** (rainbow bars, by the clock): left-click to start/stop; right-click for your last 3
  dictations (click to copy), *Retry last recording*, your vocabulary list and *Start with Windows*.
- **Types any character** (₹, é, —, emoji) on any keyboard layout. On the desktop, or in apps running as
  administrator (Windows blocks typing into those), the text goes to your clipboard instead and the popup
  says so.
- **Commands:** `dictate setup` · `dictate models` (`--use`, `--add`, `--remove`) · `dictate doctor` ·
  `dictate start` / `stop` / `toggle` / `retry` · `dictate vocab` · `dictate uninstall`
- **Where things live:** settings and vocabulary in `%APPDATA%\Dictate`, models and your last 3
  dictations in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Dictate`.

| Model | Download | Memory | 15 s of speech | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tiny.en | 78 MB | 300 MB | 0.4 s | Fastest; misses names and accents |
| base.en | 148 MB | 410 MB | 0.65 s | Light; stumbles on place names |
| **small.en** (default) | 486 MB | 850 MB | 1.7 s | Accurate with accents and names |
| medium.en | 1.5 GB | ~2 GB | ~5 s | Fewer mistakes on hard audio |
| large-v3-turbo | 1.6 GB | ~2.3 GB | ~6 s | Most accurate; slow on laptops |

Timings measured on a 4-core Intel i5-1135G7 (~ = estimate). Models are only in memory while you
dictate and are freed 90 s later.

## Linux (GNOME on Wayland)

The original version, with a GNOME Shell extension for the top-bar icon, popup and typing:

Press **Insert**, speak, press **Insert** or **Enter** → the text is typed wherever your cursor is.
A popup at the bottom center shows Listening (live waveform + timer) → Transcribing → the typed text.

- **Self-contained:** everything lives in this folder — its own Python 3.12 (`python/`, a standalone
  build), environment (`.venv/`) and model (`models/`). Nothing depends on other projects.
- **Model:** faster-whisper `small.en` (CPU, int8, 4 threads) in `models/small.en`. It loads while you
  speak; ~580 MB RAM while in use.
- **Instant results after a pause:** whenever you pause for 0.4 s, everything said so far is transcribed
  in the background as one piece. Stop after a short pause and the text is ready immediately (0.02 s in
  tests); stop mid-sentence and it takes the usual ~1.8 s. Recordings over 20 s are split at pauses.
- **Voice commands:** "new line", "new paragraph", "comma", "full stop", "question mark",
  "exclamation mark", "colon", "semicolon".
- **Vocabulary:** names and terms Whisper should prefer, one per line, in `~/.config/dictate/vocabulary.txt`.
- **Tuned on your voice:** a style hint ("clear Indian English, with proper punctuation") and no silence
  trimming inside Whisper cut word errors by about a third on both mics (earbuds 8.5% → 5.1%, laptop
  10.1% → 7.3%); vocabulary brought the laptop mic to 3.5%. Recordings with no speech skip Whisper entirely.
  Re-test any time: `.venv/bin/python tools/voice_test.py`, then `.venv/bin/python tools/benchmark.py small.en`
  (recordings stay in RAM under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/dictate-voicetest`).
- **Mic checks:** a muted, missing or zero-volume mic, a mic that can't be opened, or one that only sends
  silence each get their own message. The popup shows which mic is in use.
- **0 MB when idle:** `dictate.socket` (systemd) holds the socket and starts `dictate.service` on a
  key press (recording begins ~0.15 s later). After 90 s idle the service exits completely.
  Settings via `Environment=` in `dictate.service`: `DICTATE_MODEL` (e.g. `base.en`, downloaded to
  `models/` on first use), `DICTATE_IDLE_SECS`, `DICTATE_THREADS`, `DICTATE_MAX_SECS` (auto-finish, 300).
- **Sounds:** a soft rising chime when listening starts and a falling one when it stops
  (`dictate_core/assets/start.wav`, `stop.wav`, generated by `tools/make_sounds.py` — edit `VOLUME` there and
  rerun to change them). The first 0.35 s of each recording is dropped so the chime is never transcribed.
  Turn off with `Environment=DICTATE_SOUNDS=0` in `dictate.service`.
- **Enter** is only captured while listening; the daemon and systemd release it on stop or crash.
- **Backups (nothing is lost):**
  - No text field active (e.g. on the desktop): the text goes to the clipboard and the popup says
    "No text box was active … press Ctrl+V". Apps GNOME can't see inside (X11/Electron) get a paste too,
    and since Dictate can't tell whether a text box was active there, they get the same message; the text
    stays on the clipboard either way.
  - Right-click the top-bar icon: your last 3 dictations (click to copy; older ones are deleted), and
    **Retry last recording**. At most one recording is ever kept: the latest that failed, until retried.
  - If transcription fails, or the service crashes mid-recording, the audio is kept as
    `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/dictate.recovered.wav` for Retry (`dictate.py retry`); it's deleted once retried.
- **Privacy:** audio is recorded to `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` (RAM) and deleted after transcription. Recent
  dictations (`dictate-history.jsonl`) and a recovered recording also live only there, readable only by you,
  and vanish at logout. Transcripts are never written to logs.
- **Units:** `systemctl --user status dictate.socket dictate.service` · logs: `journalctl --user -u dictate -f`.
  The installed units are copies: after editing, run
  `cp dictate.service dictate.socket ~/.config/systemd/user/ && systemctl --user daemon-reload`.
- **Shortcuts:** GNOME Settings → Keyboard → Custom Shortcuts → "Dictate (Whisper)".
- **Top-bar button:** `gnome-extension/dictate@local` (symlinked into
  `~/.local/share/gnome-shell/extensions/`). Click the rainbow sound bars after Desktop/Apps to start/stop;
  the bars bounce and the sparkle twinkles while listening, and shimmer while transcribing (reads
  `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/dictate.state`). Changes to the extension load at the next login.
- **Typing:** through the extension's D-Bus `Type` method. Apps that use GNOME's input method get the text
  committed directly; other apps (X11/Electron) get a clipboard paste (Ctrl+Shift+V in terminals) and the
  previous clipboard is restored. Any characters work (₹, é, —, ✓). If the extension isn't loaded, it falls
  back to a virtual uinput keyboard (US layout, ASCII only; needs `70-dictate-uinput.rules` in
  `/etc/udev/rules.d/`).
- **Keys:** Insert is a GNOME Settings shortcut (works even without the extension). Enter is grabbed by
  the extension only while listening, so it can never stay stuck; without the extension the daemon falls
  back to a temporary GNOME Settings shortcut.
- **Popup:** drawn natively by the extension inside GNOME Shell (`gnome-extension/dictate@local/osd.js`,
  styled in `stylesheet.css`): soft shadow, slide-and-fade, rainbow waveform matching the icon, mic name,
  timer. The service sends it updates over D-Bus (`jeepney`, no reply awaited). Without the extension it
  falls back to `osd.py` (GTK 3 via the system Python, `/usr/bin/python3`).
- **Testing the extension without logging out:** `tools/test-shell/run.sh SCRIPT.sh` starts a hidden GNOME Shell
  with its own settings (never your live ones) and the Dictate extension loaded; scripts can `shot NAME`
  (screenshots land in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/dictate-shelltest/shots/`), drive the popup with `osd "…"`, and click
  the icon. Example: `tools/test-shell/run.sh tools/test-shell/popup_states.sh`.

## Development

```bash
python -m pytest            # core, transcription, Linux service and Windows app end to end
python tools/win_smoke.py   # Windows only: typing into a real window, shortcut, tray, popup screenshots
```

`dictate_core` is shared; `dictate.py` + `gnome-extension/` are the Linux version; `dictate_win` is the
Windows app and `dictate_cli` the `dictate` command. Licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later.
